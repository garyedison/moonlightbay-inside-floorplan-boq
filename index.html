Moonlight Bay · Inside Floor Plan & BOQ

Standardized container-home layouts and furniture packages for volume buyers. Same floor plan + same Caribbean Salt kit = better shipping density and supplier pricing.

Why one floor plan, one furniture package

When multiple buyers take the identical interior layout and the identical Caribbean Salt FF&E kit, we can:

  • Fill 40HQ containers more efficiently (fewer unique SKUs, denser packing).
  • Negotiate better factory and supplier pricing on repeated items (whitewashed oak millwork, limestone tile, linen, rattan, fixtures).
  • Ship the furniture kit in a dedicated 20 ft container DDP to the gate at a known cost.
  • Keep the exterior cladding, decks, and site work flexible while the living core stays consistent.
