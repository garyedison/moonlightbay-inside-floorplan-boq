Moonlight Bay · Inside Floor Plan & BOQ

Standardized container-home layouts and furniture packages for volume buyers. Same floor plan + same Caribbean Salt kit = better shipping density and supplier pricing.

Why one floor plan, one furniture package

When multiple buyers take the identical interior layout and the identical Caribbean Salt FF&E kit, we can fill 40HQ containers more efficiently, negotiate better factory and supplier pricing, and ship the furniture kit in a dedicated 20 ft container DDP. Exterior cladding, decks, and site work stay flexible; the living core stays locked. All plans have 8-foot ceiling heights.
